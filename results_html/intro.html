VTA→hippocampus microstructure and motivated memory

Does VTA→hippocampus white-matter microstructure relate to how accurately — and how positively — mothers remember social versus monetary feedback?

The design

55 mothers. We reconstructed the VTA→hippocampus tract — posterior and anterior, bilaterally — with CSD tractography, and profiled FA and NODDI (NDI, ODI, FWF) along it. Those along-tract measures were related to two facets of recognition memory from the feedback task — accuracy (d′) and positivity bias — for social and monetary material separately, with monetary as a within-subject specificity control. Covariates: motion, ICV, tract size, maternal age.

Extends the motivated-memory framework (Elliott et al., 2022) to individual differences in structural connectivity. Bringing it here to pressure-test where the analyses land and what's worth running next.
