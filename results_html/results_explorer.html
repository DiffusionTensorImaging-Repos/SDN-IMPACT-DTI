n = 52 social, 53 monetary. Covariates throughout: ICV, tract length, streamline count, absolute motion, maternal age. * p < .05.

Node-wise

Every node-wise permutation test that was run: 230 analyses over the two bilateral tracts (13 FWE-significant). Each row is one outcome × tract × metric. At each of the 100 nodes the outcome is regressed on that node's metric plus the covariates; contiguous nodes with p < .05 form a cluster; a cluster survives if its extent exceeds the 95th percentile of the maximum cluster under 5000 Freedman–Lane permutations. Click a row for the profile along the tract. Because the effect is diffuse, this view is descriptive; the whole-tract model above is the inferential unit.
