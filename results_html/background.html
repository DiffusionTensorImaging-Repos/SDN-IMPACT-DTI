The full measurement battery

Beyond the memory task and imaging, IMPACT collects a wide phenotype — much of it untouched here and available for future analyses. Grouped by domain:

Anxiety, mood & irritability
SCAARED (social anxiety, GAD) · IDAS-II (depression & anxiety symptom dimensions) · ARI (affective reactivity / irritability) · PSS (perceived stress)
Emotion regulation
DERS (difficulties in emotion regulation) · emotion-regulation / coping indices
Reward & motivation
BIS / BAS (behavioral inhibition & activation systems)
Childhood adversity & early environment
CTQ-SF (childhood maltreatment) · QUIC (unpredictability in childhood) · JVQ-R (juvenile victimization) · YSQ-R (early maladaptive schemas)
Aggression & personality
BPAQ-SF (Buss-Perry aggression, short form)
Parenting
PSDQ (parenting styles & dimensions)
Cognition & memory
HVLT (Hopkins Verbal Learning Test) · the experimental feedback-memory tasks — social (faces) & monetary (doors)
Psychiatric
DSM-5 cross-cutting symptom measure · psychiatric-history screen
The memory–microstructure question is one slice of a deep, well-characterized phenotype — most of the battery is untouched and open for whatever we decide to run next.
