The full measurement battery

Beyond the memory task and imaging, IMPACT collects a wide phenotype, most of it untouched here and available for future analyses.

Anxiety, mood, irritability
SCAARED (social anxiety, GAD), IDAS-II (depression and anxiety dimensions), ARI (affective reactivity), PSS (perceived stress)
Emotion regulation
DERS (difficulties in emotion regulation) and coping indices
Reward and motivation
BIS / BAS (behavioral inhibition and activation systems)
Childhood adversity
CTQ-SF (maltreatment), QUIC (unpredictability), JVQ-R (victimization), YSQ-R (early maladaptive schemas)
Aggression
BPAQ-SF (Buss-Perry aggression, short form)
Parenting
PSDQ (parenting styles and dimensions)
Cognition and memory
HVLT (verbal learning), and the experimental feedback-memory tasks: social (faces) and monetary (doors)
Psychiatric
DSM-5 cross-cutting symptom measure, psychiatric-history screen
